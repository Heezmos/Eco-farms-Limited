An Ecofarms digital product

Farmers Connect. From field to market.

Farmers Connect is the AI-powered agricultural operating system Ecofarms is developing for farms, agribusinesses, cooperatives and agricultural firms. Each organization will manage its own farms, farmers, production, harvests, marketplace, buyers, orders and operations in one secure workspace. The Ecofarms corporate website is informational; agricultural operations and commercial activity belong inside Farmers Connect.

01 / OPERATE

Run your agricultural business

Manage farms, teams, farmer networks, production cycles, inventory and harvest forecasts within your organization’s workspace.

02 / INTELLIGENCE

Get AI-assisted decisions

Bring agricultural guidance, operational insights, buyer demand and expert escalation into one accountable workflow.

03 / COMMERCE

Trade from the same system

Publish available produce and upcoming harvests, manage buyer requests, coordinate orders and record fulfillment. Cross-organization trading is planned for the next product phase.
