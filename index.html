Who We Are

Both Producer
and Platform

Eco-Farm Limited is a Private Limited Company registered in Sierra Leone, with expansion plans across West Africa. We uniquely blend food production with agritech innovation and investor-backed growth — positioning ourselves as both a producer and a platform.

Food Production

Large-scale farming of staple crops and high-value produce, with processing and distribution included.

Agritech

Smart farming tools, yield-tracking apps, logistics platforms, and digital dashboards for investors and farmers.

Agri-Investment

Structured investment opportunities where individuals and institutions fund agricultural projects and receive 10–20% ROI.

Market Analysis

A Timely Opportunity
in West Africa

Agriculture contributes over 60% to Sierra Leone's GDP and employs the majority of the population — yet the sector faces low productivity, limited capital access, and outdated practices. The opportunity is enormous.

Market Opportunity

Rising demand for food security, increasing global interest in Africa impact investment, and untapped agritech adoption potential across Sierra Leone and West Africa.

Industry Challenges

Traditional farms and cooperatives dominate but lack integrated technology and investment. NGO-funded projects exist but often lack scalability and commercial viability.

Ecofarm's Edge

A unified model combining food production, technology, and investor returns — the only platform in the region to integrate all three at scale.

Business Model

Multiple Coherent
Revenue Streams

Three revenue streams designed to de-risk the enterprise and deliver value to farmers, investors, and communities simultaneously.

Direct Produce Sales

Revenue from direct sales of produce to local and export markets, creating a reliable baseline income stream from core farming operations.

Agritech Subscriptions

Subscription and licensing fees for smart farming tools and digital platforms — a scalable, recurring revenue stream as adoption grows across the region.

Investor Profit-Sharing

Investor-backed farm projects with transparent profit-sharing, offering 10–20% ROI per cycle while contributing measurable social and food security impact.

Value Proposition: Farmers gain access to capital, technology, and markets. Investors gain transparent, measurable returns. Communities benefit from job creation and sustainable practices.
