Trust and information protection

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026

Ecofarms Limited collects only the information reasonably needed to evaluate farmer partnerships, respond to buyer supply requests and operate its agricultural services.

1. Information we collect

When you submit a farmer application, marketplace order request or supply request, we may collect your name, organization, contact details, location, farm information, products, supply requirements and any notes you choose to provide. Basic technical information may also be generated when you access the website.

2. How we use information

  • Review and respond to farmer partnership applications.
  • Assess buyer demand and agricultural supply requirements.
  • Plan verification, production, aggregation, delivery and commercial follow-up.
  • Maintain accurate operational and transaction records.
  • Respond to marketplace baskets and confirm product price, availability and delivery before an order is accepted.
  • Protect the website, prevent misuse and improve our services.

3. Website analytics

Ecofarms records privacy-conscious website activity such as page and section views, product views, navigation clicks and form starts or successful submissions. These records use a temporary session identifier and do not store visitor names, contact details or IP addresses in the analytics dataset. We use this information to understand demand, improve the website and manage services.

4. Legal and responsible use

We process information for legitimate business operations, to take steps requested by applicants or buyers, to manage commercial relationships and where necessary to meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing information

Ecofarms does not sell personal information. Information may be shared internally with authorized personnel and, when operationally necessary, with approved service providers or partners under appropriate confidentiality and security expectations. We may disclose information when required by lawful authority.

6. Storage and security

Website submissions are stored in Ecofarms’ protected database. Access is restricted through authentication, staff roles and row-level database policies. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but Ecofarms applies reasonable organizational and technical safeguards.

7. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for application review, commercial operations, record keeping, dispute prevention and legal obligations. Records that are no longer required may be securely removed or anonymized.

8. Your choices

You may ask Ecofarms to review, correct or delete information you submitted, subject to identity verification and any operational or legal retention requirement. Official contact channels will be published on this website when finalized.

9. Children

The farmer and buyer forms are intended for adults and authorized organizational representatives. They are not designed to knowingly collect information directly from children.

10. Policy updates

We may update this policy as Ecofarms’ services and legal obligations develop. The effective date on this page identifies the current published version.

This policy describes the present website and application workflows. Additional notices may apply when future marketplace, payment, farmer-portal or delivery services are introduced.
